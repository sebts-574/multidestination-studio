# Multidestination Studio 1.1.9

## New: frames
- **+** in the Sources dock → **Frame…**: an empty frame (name, width, height) added to the active destination, centred. It is shown as a dotted outline in the studio only.
- **Drop files from your computer onto a frame**: they go into the frame, fitted with their proportions kept and centred.
- A frame is an OBS group: click inside it to select it, move or resize it, its contents follow. Its size can also be changed in the properties of its frame source.
- Companion: the Group Source Selector of a frame cycles through its files.

## Startup
- With **Launch Multidestination Studio on startup**, the studio now opens in full screen on macOS too.

## Companion
- Module: `obs-studio-4.0.0-multidestination.3.tgz` (unchanged).

## Installation
- **Windows 10/11**: `multidestination-studio-1.1.9-windows-x64-installer.exe` (not code-signed: SmartScreen may ask for confirmation).
- **macOS**: universal `.pkg` (not signed: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway).
- **Ubuntu 24.04**: `multidestination-studio-1.1.9-x86_64-linux-gnu.deb` (`sudo apt install ./multidestination-studio-1.1.9-x86_64-linux-gnu.deb`).
- **User guide (PDF)**: [Multidestination-Studio-1.2.1-User-Guide.pdf](https://github.com/sebts-574/multidestination-studio/blob/main/docs/Multidestination-Studio-1.2.1-User-Guide.pdf) (latest guide).

Close OBS before installing. Requires OBS Studio 31.1 or later.
